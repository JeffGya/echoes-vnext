## Top 3 Risks & Mitigation Strategies

### Risk 1: Discovery & Market Saturation (LIKELIHOOD: MEDIUM, IMPACT: HIGH)

**Problem:**
- Mobile app stores oversaturated (5M+ apps iOS, 3M+ Android)
- Indie game discovery challenging (algorithm-dependent)
- Strategy game category competitive but not oversaturated
- Echoes unknown indie brand (no franchise recognition)

**Mitigation Strategy:**

| Action | Timeline | Budget | Expected ROI |
|--------|----------|--------|--------------|
| Pre-launch PR push | 6 months | $100K | 50+ press mentions |
| Creator seeding | 3 months | $80K | 500+ total streams |
| Community building | 12 months | $50K | 5K Discord members |
| App Store optimization | 3 months | $30K | Top 50 strategy category |
| Content marketing | Ongoing | $40K/year | Organic search traffic |
| **TOTAL** | | **$300K** | Estimated 200K+ impressions |

**Success Metric:** 50K+ active players within 3 months of launch (achievable with above spend)

### Risk 2: Onboarding & Narrative Complexity (LIKELIHOOD: MEDIUM, IMPACT: MEDIUM)

**Problem:**
- Echoes feature-rich: emotion systems, calling mechanics, vectors, bonds, Threads
- Risk: Overwhelms casual players
- Narrative games lose 40% players in first hour (industry standard)
- Complex systems need excellent UX presentation

**Mitigation Strategy:**

1. **Tiered Onboarding**
   - Hour 1: Introduce Sanctum + 1-2 mechanics (emotion, party)
   - Hour 2: Introduce first Realm + combat (determinism as teaching moment)
   - Hour 3+: Introduce calling/vector depth (optional depth layers)
   - Avoid: Tutorial overload (max 15 min continuous tutorial)

2. **Difficulty/Accessibility Options**
   - "Explore" mode: No permadeath, lower difficulty, story focus
   - "Challenge" mode: Permadeath variant, higher difficulty
   - "Story" mode: Narrative focus, minimal mechanical challenge
   - Accessibility: Text scaling, colorblind modes, control rebinds

3. **In-Game Guidance**
   - Context-sensitive tooltips
   - Glossary system (for Akan terms, game concepts)
   - "Why" explanations (not just "what")
   - Optional guided tours

4. **Testing & Iteration**
   - Beta testing: Focus on Hour 1 experience (critical)
   - Collect dropout data: Where do players quit?
   - A/B test onboarding flow
   - Polish until 70%+ players complete Hour 1

**Success Metric:** 60%+ players reach first Realm (achievable with good onboarding)

### Risk 3: Cultural Misrepresentation Backlash (LIKELIHOOD: LOW, IMPACT: CRITICAL)

**Problem:**
- Akan cultural foundation is core to Echoes
- Risk: Inauthentic execution undermines entire premise
- Diaspora community will critique authenticity closely
- Single cultural misstep can damage brand irreparably

**Mitigation Strategy:**

1. **Ongoing Cultural Consultation**
   - Establish cultural advisory board (2-3 Akan/Ghanaian cultural experts)
   - Monthly consultation during development
   - Review all Akan language, names, concepts
   - Budget: $5K-10K annually

2. **Community Transparency**
   - Be open about development decisions
   - Explain cultural choices in dev blog
   - Acknowledge when uncertain (don't fake authenticity)
   - Commit to post-launch refinement based on feedback

3. **Beta Community Engagement**
   - Invite diaspora players to closed beta (50-100)
   - Specifically request cultural feedback
   - Make visible changes based on feedback
   - Credit cultural contributors

4. **Launch Messaging**
   - Frame as "made with cultural respect, not appropriation"
   - Highlight Akan language elements
   - Acknowledge that Echoes is creative interpretation, not documentary
   - Make cultural grounding visible (not hidden)

5. **Contingency Plan**
   - If major backlash occurs: pause launch, gather feedback, iterate
   - Prepare public statement acknowledging concerns
   - Budget for rapid iteration (2 weeks max)

**Success Metric:** 80%+ positive cultural feedback in beta (achievable with above steps)

- Create content: Design deep-dives on emotion system, calling mechanics

**Phase 2: Beta & Press (3 months before launch)**

**Phase 3: Launch Window (Q3 2026)**
- Simultaneous iOS + Android global launch
- Coordinate press embargo (day of launch)
- Content creator livestreams coordinated
- Social media blitz (TikTok, Twitter, Reddit threads)
- Community events (Discord fireside chats)

**Phase 4: Post-Launch Momentum (Months 1-6)**
- Week 1: Monitor critical path (bugs, crashes, balance)
- Week 2-4: First content update (balance tweaks, quality-of-life)
- Month 2: First story event (new realm or calling option)
- Month 3-6: Ongoing updates + community engagement
- Month 6: Evaluate Switch/PC porting decisions

### Platform Release Order

**Recommended:** Mobile-first, then PC, then console

1. **Q3 2026 (Launch):** iOS + Android global
2. **Q4 2026 (4-6 weeks later):** Steam PC (polish from mobile feedback)
3. **Q1 2027 (3+ months):** Nintendo Switch (port quality check)
4. **Q2+ 2027 (optional):** PlayStation/Xbox (business case dependent)

**Rationale:**
- Mobile is largest addressable market + easiest to ship
- PC launch gives console ports time for quality
- Staggered release = sustained visibility
- Avoids "launching everywhere at once" risk
