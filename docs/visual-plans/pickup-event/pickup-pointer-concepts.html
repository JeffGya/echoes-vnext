Pickup pointer: concepts to compare (prototype, not the game)

Cutscene moment: the game decides who goes first. Each concept plays the same racetrack path and the same timing. Board, tokens and cards are drawn on a 1280x720 canvas that scales to the window. Colours and sizes are estimates. This page is not the game.

A. The Stage. Made of: one dark scrim (#201613) over the tiles, one soft ivory light pool (2:1 ellipse, bright core, no outline). Others dim to 75%. NEW: one scrim layer.
B. The Drifting Fragment. Made of: one ivory mote above the heads, a 5-sample trail, a ground shadow, a halo behind the head, a short brightness lift. No scrim. NEW: nothing.
C. The Lens. Made of: a screen vignette, a small camera offset toward the pointer, a small ivory core light, a 5% push-in on landing. NEW: vignette and camera offset.
