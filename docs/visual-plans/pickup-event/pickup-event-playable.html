Notes: what each part proposes and what to judge

All names, barks and lines in this file are written by the agents as a proposal. Jeff decides the wording.

What you can do

  • Watch the intro, or press Skip, or tap anywhere in the frame. Skip never changes the result.
  • Click, tap or press Enter on any item: above the head, on the ground or in the pack. Details float next to it and the intro waits while they are open. Esc, Close or a click outside closes them.
  • When the pack is full and an item is left, details show Swap. Press it, click the item to trade with, read the preview, then press Swap them or Keep as it is.
  • Press Continue. With Scout Carefully you get one question if the swap is still on offer. With Seek Signs you do not.
  • Items start as sealed bundles. They flip when the Echo touches them. Items that are left flip when the Echo finishes. A sealed item cannot be selected.
  • The pack icons (swap and destroy) show what is left on this path. Hover or focus shows the full line. The destroy is shown only.
  • The swap is one per path. It stays spent across Play again. Reset the path makes it new. Changing the scenario also starts a new path.

What to judge

  • Does the pointer read as a light that passes the Echoes and slows to rest, without looking like a spinner or a mark?
  • Is the chosen Echo clear (steps forward, name tag, others dim and step back) without any ring?
  • Does the pack panel arriving with the chooser tell you what is at stake? Is the full tone enough without numbers?
  • Is the emotion beat (pose and one bark) readable without a meter? Compare scenario 1 (plus), 3 (minus) and 10 (no extra minus).
  • Do floating details help or get in the way? Do they ever cover something you need?
  • Is the swap state clear? One marker on the legal targets, everything else dim. Does the preview say what is lost?
  • Is the intro too long in repeat play? Scenarios 5, 6 and 9 are the slowest on purpose. Use 2x and Skip to judge.

What this page does not claim

  • Chooser, picks, reason and cases come from fixture data and small pure functions. The game must supply the real values (decision D-06).
  • Timings come from the feel report. Where the new event differs I chose values in the same language. They are proposals.
  • The pack icons carry the swap allowance. There is no marker on the floor. The details description lines are placeholders. No audio plays.
  • "Resolve opens here." is a stand-in. It is not a design.
  • Marks are counted by kind on revealed items: grade pips, kind shape, twin notch. The pack icons are UI, not marks. The leaf on pack tiles and the count capsule on stacks come from the approved pack tiles and are outside this count.
