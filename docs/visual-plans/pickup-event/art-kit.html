0. The path view today (what Concept C must fit)

  • The path view is an isometric clay board of 128x64 cells (StageExploreScreen.tscn, a TileMapLayer in BoardRoot).
  • It shows one party token: a blue disc with one letter, drawn by PartyTokenLayer.gd. It does not show the single Echoes.
  • Route: a ghost trail of footprints behind the token. Unseen cells have a black layer at 65%.
  • Nodes: round markers on cells. Hidden is grey, revealed is blue, resolved is flat grey at 55%. Over the board float a step bar, a directive badge, a pack button and a bark layer.
  • Not seen: I did not run the game. I read the scene and the scripts only. I viewed the clay tile PNG but did not measure it. Concept C needs the single Echo tokens to exist at a node. Today they do not. The ring is therefore new drawing: the party token opens into its Echoes.

1.2 Light and depth

  1. One pool of light. A soft vignette darkens the board edges (dark brown at 72%). The light sits on the finds and the drawn Echo. Everything else sits in the dark part.
  2. The ground is flat and quiet. Cell edges stay at 17%, as in the combat mockup. Nothing on the ground competes with a find.
  3. Depth by the foot point. Objects sort by their ground y. Every object has a contact shadow on the ground. The shadow tells where the object really is.
  4. Finds are slabs, not cards. Each find has a 5 unit dark lower edge, like a carved tile, and a ground shadow. It has no glow and no bordered panel. This is the main fix against "webby".

1.4 The one swap mark

The swap mark is two curved arrows that chase each other, drawn flat on the ground (cream #FFF8DC, 3.5 wide, with a 7.5 wide ink keyline). It lies on the ground between the Echo and the left finds. It is one mark because the Keeper has one swap per path. One mark means one chance. It shows no count and no "used" version. It shows only while a swap is possible. It disappears when the swap is spent or skipped. It uses no circle disc, no cross and no blue. The shape is not an Adinkra form, so no cultural sign is borrowed or invented.

Swap action: the Keeper drags a left find onto a carried find. Tap-left-then-tap-carried also works. The two finds trade places on a 0.6 s arc. The left find rises on an upper arc. The carried find falls on a lower arc.

Honest limits

  • Too close: pose 3 (pausing) and pose 6 (stiff). Both stand still. Only the tremble separates them, and a still frame shows no tremble.
  • Too close: pose 4 (striding) and pose 7 (easy sweep). Both move forward. They differ by stretch and trail (bold) against a gentle arc plus lifted finds (easy). Keep the bold stride longer than the party spacing so it cannot pass for an easy sweep.
  • Also close: pose 1 (stepping out) and pose 5 (close to the line). The route to the find makes the difference, not the pose.
  • A letter disc cannot show: eyes, a glance, hands, shoulders. "Froze" and "saw its worth" need these. The disc carries them only by timing (stop length, tremble) and by what the finds do. This is a limit of the placeholder art, not an art mistake.

Deviations from the combat mockup style

  • New slab objects with the 001a tile colours on a dark board. The combat mockup has no loot object.
  • One new value: the left plate #CDB791.
  • Other tokens drop to 65% opacity while one Echo acts. Combat does not do this.
  • The vignette is stronger than the combat floor. It exists only during the event.
  • The brief says "letter on a coloured square". The real tokens (combat mockup and PartyTokenLayer.gd) are discs. I drew discs.

7. Risks for Jeff

  1. Webby risk. The cream plate is still a rounded rectangle. If the slab edge, tilt and ground shadow are cut for time, it becomes a card on a field. Do not cut them. The bark bubble is also a UI shape. Reuse the existing bark layer.
  2. Letter tokens. The pose set carries "bold", "easy", "close" and "safe". It does not carry "froze" or "saw its worth" in a still view. Both need timing.
  3. Token against ground. The token fill and outline are weak against the clay (1.09:1 and 2.28:1). The letter carries identity. A cream keyline on the focused token would help. That changes combat. I did not do it.
  4. Concept C needs new art logic. Single Echo tokens do not exist on the path view. The party token must open into them.
  5. Concept A reads faster. Concept C teaches two Echoes at once. I did not rank them. That is for Jeff.
  6. Left finds are quiet. At 3.17:1 against the ground they are readable but dim. The Keeper must still read them to swap. If play tests show misses, use a lighter left plate (#E0CDAE gives 3.97:1).
