Grounding and limits

Emotion flash backgrounds reuse the Sanctum colour mapping: EchoCardItem → EmotionPresentation → LivingTreeSystem’s EmotionStatus font colours. Settled chips stay cream; flashes use ink text on Radiant, black on Whole and Uncertain, and cream on the remaining colours for contrast.

The 10 labels follow EmotionPresentation and EmotionService; EncounterSnapshotBuilder supplies projected emotion, HP and bark fields. Hit, Echo-only kill relief and ally-loss examples follow the combat action and round-emotion sources; support has a source cue only where an actual action is supplied. Self-regulation and passive calming illustrate an exposed status outcome without claiming its hidden cause has a dedicated event.

Displacement resistance follows MovementHazardService and LiveMovementContextService: Whole-gated Position Lock protects its owner, while Anchor Presence protects nearby living Echo allies. The resolved Unstable event keeps the same cell and zero damage, but its current player-facing hazard/result presentation has a gap, so FEEL-15 is conditional. It grants neither Guard nor melee immunity.

The approved starter Echo portrait and character-style canvas were inspected for vulnerability, hesitation and grounded commitment. Geometry reuses the requester-restored rigid Actions iteration: regular frames and board, circle/square tokens, rectangular HP and chips, circular turn cue. Barks are existing authored lines, including high-signal fear and the supported ally reply context.
