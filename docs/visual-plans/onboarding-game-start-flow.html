UX friction + opportunities

Where the emotional thread can loosen

These are player-facing audit findings. They are not implementation authorization.

Friction · framing

Two different starter premises

The live flow lets the player choose the fragment that creates the starter Echo. The GDD summary says one Echo is already present. These establish different kinds of responsibility.

Friction · causality

The Flame wakes twice in meaning

The current state marks the Ase Flame awakened when the house is named, before the First Trial, then presents an Awakening Rite after the Thread returns. The GDD’s intended causal story is clearer: recover → return → visibly awaken.

Friction · pacing

Long runway before agency

Invocation and Anansi are atmosphere-first. If typing and continue beats run too long, the first meaningful choice arrives after attention has cooled.

Friction · attachment

The meeting risks becoming a character sheet

Standing, Step, Storyweight, archetype, identity, emotion, traits and stats all appear immediately. The person may be understood as a build before being felt as unsettled.

Friction · stakes

Protected can feel consequence-free

The rewind is a powerful Anansi beat, but only if it teaches that this mercy is exceptional and future Realm loss is real.

Friction · feedback

Promise consequences are subtle

Guard, listen, and return change emotion or virtue under the hood. Without a visible Echo response, three evocative choices can feel equivalent.

Friction · handoff

The hub can flatten the climax

After a focused mythic sequence, landing in a broad navigation rail can feel like exiting a story into a dashboard unless the changed house is foregrounded.

Opportunity · response

Make the Echo answer the promise

Add a short reactive line or posture change that reflects guard, listen, or return before showing any numeric shift.

Opportunity · continuity

Use one repeated spatial composition

Show the same Sanctum center dormant, ember-lit, then awakened so restoration is perceived rather than explained.

Opportunity · focus

Progressively reveal identity

Lead the meeting with name, emotional status, one identity phrase, and one bark. Defer detailed stats until the player reaches Echo management.

Recommended experience direction

Keep the implemented structure; sharpen its visible causality

The existing flow already carries the right mythic verbs. The highest-value direction is to connect each decision to a visible person or place response.

Current experience

The player chooses a fragment, name, promise, and weave. Much of the meaning is explained in copy or reflected in underlying state.

Sharpened experience

Choice → visible response → changed space → next screen

Every meaningful act receives one immediate emotional response and one persistent environmental or identity echo.

  • Fragment: voice becomes person
  • Promise: Echo and Flame answer
  • Trial: wound yields material
  • Weave: before/after identity is readable
  • Handoff: changed Sanctum leads the eye

Open question · premise

Is the starter Echo chosen into existence or already waiting?

This decides whether the player’s first responsibility is answering a voice or caring for someone already present. The current implementation strongly supports the first.

Open question · boundary

Does onboarding end at “Keep the House” or at summoning unlock?

The implemented gate ends at Sanctum entry; the GDD defines start + tutorial as everything before summoning opens. The handoff guidance and playtest length depend on this boundary.

Open question · consequence

How visibly should the Keeper promise persist?

Should guard/listen/return become recurring player-facing house language, or remain a small opening tilt? Persistence changes expectation and narrative weight.
