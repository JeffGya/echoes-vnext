Combat clarity

Approved and locked · 22 October 2026 · animated playback loops automatically.

Watch the acting actor, or select another actor to inspect its latest result this round. Health stays on the board.

Reference player

Authored demonstration · prebattle. All actor values and action timings are illustrative.

One place for each read

The objective leads. The sidebar owns names, emotion and acting/selected action results. All actors use matching HP on the field. Gold means acting; blue means camera selection.

Watch, then look closer

Combat advances automatically. Select a token or sidebar item to follow that actor. Empty ground clears focus. Camera selection changes no target or action. Panning or zooming temporarily suspends follow; empty ground releases it.

A short, complete handoff

Finish the action, group the round boundary, then move to Resolve. No intermediate End Combat button. Back retains its current navigation meaning.

Research and design choices

Artist, UI/UX and game-feel agents reviewed these together using the game-design skill. Screenshot observations and design inferences are separated below.

  • XCOM 2 and Gears Tactics: inspected screenshots place compact information around a full battlefield. We borrow hierarchy, not their command bars.
  • Desperados III and Shadow Gambit: inspected screenshots use small crew identities and local world cues. The selected reference uses sparse composition; the tutorial examples do not prove dense battle readability.
  • The Last Flame, The Dungeon Beneath and Astronarch: developer descriptions establish autonomous party combat and positioning as closer mechanical comparisons. Our inference is to emphasize resolved consequences and make detail inspectable; this is not a claim about visually inspected HUDs.
  • Battle Brothers: developer documentation supports consistent onboard health. Darkest Dungeon II makes psychological state consequential; we keep Echoes’ own emotion vocabulary and expressive responses.

Selected direction: Jeffrey approved and locked the final compact Observer HUD as the inventory reference on 22 October 2026. The sidebar keeps names and exposed emotion visible, with action results for the acting and camera-selected actors only.

Readability checks: review seven actors at Fast, current ≠ selected, a missed refusal, stop-short settlement, death and overlapping barks. Check that names, emotion and the actual outcome remain easy to read.

Art direction: all outer sidebar panels and nested row cards are removed. Cream text, restrained ink shadows, familiar emotion colors and gold/blue emphasis sit directly over the clay field; screen/card geometry and actor tokens stay regular.
