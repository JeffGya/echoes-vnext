# Storybook combat encounter — accepted visual reference

Jeffrey approved and locked this study on 13 November 2026. “This is great and give a real feel. Lock this in.”

Reference: [Storybook encounter](combat-actors-objects-study.html#storybook-title)

The approved direction covers the animated Purify Shrine encounter. The Echoes walk with planted steps, wind up and follow through on strikes, brace for hits, and settle into quiet idles. The Dust Wanderer and Vale Brute have distinct non-human silhouettes, gaits, and attacks. The 2:1 isometric board uses painted clay, cliffs, a rope bridge, and restrained foliage and dust motion.

The encounter carries forward the destination cue through travel and clears it on arrival. The floating Observer shows names, emotion, and action results, while health and signed damage sit on the board. Guard is distinct from targeting. Warder's Vigil raises Guard on the receiving Echo, Steward's Call shows the receiving Echo settle, and Purify reduces subsequent Shrine drain without restoring health.

The approval is for a scripted visual reference with playback controls. Its timing, positions, and HP values belong to the study. The static comparisons cover additional actors, objects, and states for review.
