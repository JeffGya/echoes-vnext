Grounding and current limits

Current objective snapshots and CombatBoardScreen own instruction and progress. CombatState resolves victory and defeat; enemy elimination can finish most objective modes, while Endure can continue until its waves are spawned and Guide Spirit has its own spirit-first checks. Purify stacks reduce future drain through ShrineService and never restore HP; surviving the shrine and defeating opposition can win, without an invented purification threshold.

Recover adjacency, Protect guard and Pursue containment lose their consecutive progress when proximity is lost. Guide Spirit protect instead accumulates progress and pauses out of range; its HP and rounds remaining occupy the spirit card, while its banner carries the instruction or Arrived. A noncombatant spirit uses the current square body with a spirit halo; a joined spirit uses an Echo circle.

Recover has a one-time holder designation, but the current objective snapshot has no holder or carrier identity, so the conditional identity proposal receives no live badge or succession animation. Protect theft and clearing theft are projected, while carrying/pickup motion is not established by the current live path; the totem remains fixed. Charge pressure is one banner line, without an ambient warning loop.

Pace is currently integrated through PaceService, the encounter snapshot, CombatBoardScreen and PacePresentation. Its existing Full / Partial / None colours tint the compact round read and use one brief brightening on a drop; the warm result surface uses its corresponding darker palette.
