## How to read the lines

- `FRAME SPIKE`: one frame took 28 ms or more. The text names the last action.
- `ACTION TOTAL`: one UI action: `dispatch` + `render` + `flush`.
- `STAGE ADVANCE` and `COMBAT STEP`: phase times inside `dispatch`, in ms. `xN` is the call count of a function in that step.
- The accumulators (`reachable_region`, `shortest_path`, `find_explore_target`) are also counted inside the phase that called them.
