- **D-01 Scope.** 001b is part of the ITEM-001 Notion row. No new row. The path pack view shows gathered finds (closes gap F8-02). (Jeff, 2026-11-10)

(Jeff, 2026-11-10)

- **D-47 Finds are a chance within the cap.** Getting finds up to the cap is a chance, not a given. The chance is a base percentage, with a modifier on top for how well the encounter went. - **D-48 Finds are a rolling chance, falling per find.** The number of finds in a fight is a rolling chance. Each possible find, from the first up to the cap, rolls its own chance. The first find has the highest chance, the second less, then the third, the fourth, the fifth and the sixth, and so on. The encounter rank (D-47) is a modifier that raises the chance of each find. Most encounters are close to certain to give the first find, and have a decent but lower chance for the second. The cap is a possibility, not a guarantee. Open: the base chance per find, the modifier per rank, where the rule lives (001b or ITEM-002), and the seeded stream. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-49 001b builds the finds chance rule (exception to D-29).** 001b builds the chance function, its config block (`data.inventory.finds_chance`), its validator and its probe, together with the cap. - **D-72 The pickup event runs on the real board (2026-11-17, U5 review).** (a) The event uses the real board tokens on real tiles and the existing bark popup (`BarkPopupLayer`). The rule: walkable and free tiles of the largest region, 2 to 3 tiles (Chebyshev) from the row centre, lowest hash of `[seed, situation_id, run_count, find_id, col, row]`, at least 2 tiles between finds, fallbacks relax spacing, widen distance, use occupied tiles, then pile. (c) The Echoes form a row on tiles: a straight line of N adjacent free tiles near the party centre, Echoes in snapshot `row` order from screen-left to screen-right, line tie broken by hash, fallback the N nearest free tiles as a chain, or stay on their own tiles. Each Echo walks on the board's own shortest path. (d) The event draws only the standing Echoes. Dead enemies and fallen Echoes are not drawn. (e) A find is the 001a `ItemTile` at the tile centre with a ground ellipse from the token shadow config. No new theme variation.
