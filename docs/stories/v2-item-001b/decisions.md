# V2-ITEM-001b decisions

Written in STE. Project-wide rule: `ANSWERS.md` #91 (Echo decisions follow one readable pattern).

- **D-01 Scope.** 001b is part of the ITEM-001 Notion row. No new row. The path pack view shows gathered finds (closes gap F8-02). (Jeff, 2026-11-10)
- **D-02 Test source.** No real drops. The F1 command `inventory produce gather` grows: grades in its config copy, several finds per call, a named Standing, a named fallen Echo. (Jeff, 2026-11-10)
- **D-03 Swap and destroy.** Separate allowances. One swap per path (`swap_used`) and one destroy per path (`discard_used`). The swap takes one left find and drops one gathered find. It never drops a held piece. (Jeff, 2026-11-10)
- **D-04 Clearing.** The left list and `swap_used` clear at an early leave, at defeat and at the path end. (Jeff, 2026-11-10)
- **D-05 Chooser.** After each won fight one Echo is drawn with equal chance from the Echoes that did not fall, in party order. The draw happens even when all finds fit. Stream: `inventory.pick.<stage_id>.<run_count>.<situation_id>`. Never `stage.settle`. A saved pick stamp makes it exactly-once. (Jeff, 2026-11-10)
- **D-06 Pick rule (Option 1).** The chooser scores each find: identity (Calling family, vectors, traits, stats, archetype), sense (grade, kind, quantity, scaled by Judgment) and state (afraid, bold, Calling signature). Sort by score, tie to the lower `find_id`. No RNG after the chooser draw. Standing, Storyweight, bonds and vows reach the pick only through Judgment and composure. Follows `ANSWERS.md` #91. (Jeff, 2026-11-10)
- **D-07 Freeze gate.** A chooser whose afraid value is at or above `freeze_at` takes the finds in find order. The reason is "froze". (Jeff, 2026-11-10)
- **D-08 UI scope.** In: the pickup moment on the encounter card; the left row and swap on the path pack panel. Out: departure modal, the free-slot floor, discard of left items. Panel placement stays with Jeff's UX redesign. (Jeff, 2026-11-10)
- **D-09 Numbers.** `mid-game-designer` proposes all `data.inventory.pick` values in the chat. Jeff approves. A rate probe must show a measurable difference between a Standing 1 and a Standing 9 chooser (LESSONS #38). (Jeff, 2026-11-10)
- **D-10 Text.** Jeff approves every player-facing line (ANSWERS #89).
- **D-11 Swap origin.** The swap comes from 001a D-14 (2026-10-27). Jeff confirmed the swap on 2026-11-10 and separated it from the destroy.
- **D-12 Numbers approved.** Jeff approved the `data.inventory.pick` numbers proposed by `mid-game-designer` on 2026-11-10 (calling, archetype, vector, trait, stat, state, sense and freeze tables; `freeze_at` 0.5; Calling-keyed `calling_pick` with 6 rows; six stats without speed; trait and vector sums scaled by Judgment as in combat; `afraid_pick_signature` beside `afraid_signature`; `freeze_at` compared with the curved afraid). They are written to `data/balance.json` in the backend step, after the mockup is approved. The rate probe may still send them back (D-09).
- **D-13 Design on hold.** The UX choices C1 to C9 are undecided. Jeff wants a visual mockup before he decides. The art and feel rounds wait for the mockup.
- **D-14 No persistent left list (supersedes D-04 and the "left row" part of D-08).** What the Echoes leave is visible only in the pickup event right after the encounter. It is not shown at any other time. The Travel pack panel shows only what is in the Travel pack. There is no left row, no left cue on the HUD and no left count. (Jeff, 2026-11-10: "That allows too many opportunities to make choices and get the perfect item.")
- **D-15 The swap happens only in the pickup event (changes D-03).** After an encounter is the only swap opportunity and the only time the player sees the loot of that encounter. The Keeper has ONE swap per PATH. It is spent only inside a pickup event, so the player chooses when to use it. (Jeff, 2026-11-10)
- **D-16 The pickup is an event before Resolve.** Right after the encounter, an event shows what the Echo picked and what it left. Then Resolve shows the final result, including the items the party goes away with. This replaces the "section on the win card" idea (C2 option 1). (Jeff, 2026-11-10)
- **D-17 No history.** No habit strip, no history card, no pick log (answers C1: marks only; a history card goes against the GDD). (Jeff, 2026-11-10)
- **D-18 Fewer glyphs.** Reduce the glyph set. The mockup must not become a mess of icons. (Jeff, 2026-11-10)
- **D-19 It must feel like a game.** The v3 mockup is too webby. The redesign starts from research on similar games (XCOM, Gears Tactics, Tomodachi Life and Friends). Jeff names these as the spiritual and conceptual references of the game. (Jeff, 2026-11-10)
- **D-20 Concept A chosen, revised.** Jeff prefers Concept A (end of combat, on the board). The first animated mockup was "just visual" and too limited: it ignored UI, gameplay and the pack. The next mockup must be interactive and playable, with scenarios. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-21 The chooser is shown by a pointer.** After the battle the Echoes stand in a row. A pointer or similar marker passes along them, slows down and selects one. It looks random (the result is the seeded draw of D-05). The chosen Echo then steps forward and chooses items on the field. This replaces the plain reveal. Jeff does not want the spinning swap icon of the first mockup. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-22 The pack shows during the event.** While the Echo chooses, the pack comes up. The player sees what is in it and whether it is full. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-23 Emotion effect (direction, rules and numbers still to design).** Pack full and the Echo cannot choose: a small emotional impact on that Echo. Empty pack and all its picks fit: a positive effect (a little more radiant). Only some picks fit: a neutral response. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-24 Swap only when the pack is full (changes D-15).** If there is room, items go into the pack at once. If the pack is full and items remain, the player may swap. The count of swaps per path is open. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-25 Item details everywhere in the event.** The player can select any item (above the Echo's head, on the field, in the pack) and see its details floating next to it, with an image and details, like the Sanctum details panel. The details have a Swap button. Pressing it opens a swap state in which the player picks the other item to swap with. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-26 Open design questions Jeff asked.** (a) Does the event play right after battle or after the result screen? D-16 says right after the battle, before Resolve. (b) Are items on the field during combat, or do they replace where the enemies stood? (c) What happens when the objective is achieved without killing enemies: are locations chosen at random? (b) and (c) are open and go to the design round. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-27 Swap count.** The Keeper has ONE swap per PATH. It is offered only in a pickup event when the pack is full and items are left on the field (confirms D-15, completes D-24). (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-28 Swap drops any pack item (changes D-03).** The swap may drop a gathered item or a held piece. The destroy action (one per path, D-33 of 001a) stays as it is. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-29 Story split accepted as a WORKING DRAFT.** 001b keeps: the pointer (plain highlight first), the pick rule, the pack panel in the event, the details panel with placeholders, swap only when full, one swap per path, the event state before Resolve, Resolve showing final items, the debug producer and scenarios, and the interactive prototype. Later stories: real placement on the board and no-kill anchors (a new "Pickup placement on the board" story), the emotion effect (a new "Pickup reaction effect" story; 001b computes only a reaction class for pose and bark), the image and description of items (V2-ITEM-002), equipped items (V2-ITEM-003), recycle of a dropped item (V2-ITEM-004), crafting use of materials (V2-ECONOMY-003), pack size by progression (V2-CONTINUITY-002). Seams S1 to S6 hold. Part 9 follows D-28 (swap drops any pack item), not the agent's narrower set. Story codes come from the backlog CSV and must be checked in Notion before any row is changed. (Jeff, 2026-11-12: "Accept as draft")
- **D-30 Concept document.** When the rules are solid, a cross-story concept document is written in `docs/` (12 headings proposed) for all ITEM stories to reference. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-31 Items appear only at the end.** Loot items appear on the board only when the pickup event starts (after the win). Nothing is on the board during combat. Combat cells and AI do not change. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-32 No-kill placement (rule for the prototype and the later placement story).** For kills, an item lies where the enemy fell. When the objective is won without killing every enemy, items lie at the objective's own cell (shrine, spirit's end cell, relic tile, quarry, protected entity). Extra items go to the nearest free cells. No random numbers. `endure` has no anchor and uses the party centre. The real placement logic belongs to the later placement story (D-29). (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-33 Positive emotion boost (changes the literal reading of D-23).** The chosen Echo gets a small positive effect whenever everything it picks fits the pack, empty or partly full (Option Y). It adds to the +10 morale and -5 fear that every win already gives. The effect belongs to the later "Pickup reaction effect" story (D-29). (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-34 Afraid chosen Echo.** An Echo in a severe emotion band (burdened or worse, or frozen) gets no extra minus in the full-pack case. It may still get the plus when everything fits (F2). (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-35 Leave guard tied to Directives.** When the player presses Continue while a swap is still on offer and items remain, a careful Directive asks once "Leave these behind?" and a rash or speedy Directive does not. It is one data field per Directive (for example `confirm_leave_items`). Scout Carefully is true. Seek Signs is false. A future Directive sets its own value. No Directive name appears in `core/` logic (ANSWERS #81). (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-36 Playtest verdict on the playable mockup.** "It has a good feel. It feels like a real choice, and it feels like something that can impact strategically and help me in the game play and choose." The structure of the playable mockup stands. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-37 Items are hidden until chosen.** Items on the board show as a generic icon that does not say what the item is, and not in a card. An item is revealed when the Echo chooses it. The items that are left are revealed after the picks, when the result comes, or when nothing can be held. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-38 Softer glow.** The glow (the pointer light and similar effects) is good but too heavy. It becomes subtler. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-39 A frozen chooser does not choose (supersedes D-07).** A frozen chooser selects nothing. The player loses the chance to get items that round. This is the consequence of freezing, so there is no emotion minus (consistent with D-34). Open: whether the swap is still offered, and what is revealed. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-40 Logical cap on finds, scaled by realm.** The number of finds per event has a cap. The cap scales with the realm (realm scale, which scales with difficulty): the later game has more finds than the early game. Seven finds on three cells was too much. The numbers go to `mid-game-designer` and live in config (ANSWERS #83). (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-41 Full pack: the item stays near the Echo.** When the pack is full the item still floats above or near the chosen Echo, with the bark, and it is clear that the player can swap. The bark must not say "hands are full": it says the pack is full. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-42 No swap marker on the floor.** The swap marker leaves the board. An icon on the Travel pack shows that the player has a swap and a destroy left. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-43 Frozen chooser: no swap.** A frozen chooser selects nothing and the swap is not offered that round. The player loses the round's items. They are revealed and then lost when the event ends. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-44 Destroy is shown, not used, in the event.** The Travel pack icon shows the swap and destroy that are left. The destroy works on the path view as in 001a (one per path). In the event the swap is the only tool. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-45 Hidden items are not selectable.** A hidden item on the board cannot be selected. It becomes selectable when it is revealed. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-46 Cap values approved.** Cap on finds per pickup event: realm scale 1.0 (realm.01 and the prologue) = 2; scale 1.25 (realm.02) = 3. The later bands (1.75 = 4, 2.5 = 5, 3.5 = 6) are placeholders for realms that do not exist yet. Key: `data.inventory.finds_cap_by_realm_scale`, an ordered list of bands. Open: Option A (the seam cuts) or Option B (sources respect the cap, the seam refuses `over_cap`), pack size growing with the cap, and a limit by free board cells. (Jeff, 2026-11-12: "Yes approve")
- **D-47 Finds are a chance within the cap.** Getting finds up to the cap is a chance, not a given. The chance is a base percentage, with a modifier on top for how well the encounter went. The existing fight grade (F, D, C, B, A, S: win 0.4 + Echoes alive 0.4 + pace 0.2, `RewardCalc`) is the reading of "how well the encounter went" (Jeff confirmed: "We have encounter ranking."), so there is one reading. Open: per-find rolls or one count roll, the base percentage, the modifier per grade, where the rule lives (001b or ITEM-002), and the seeded stream. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-48 Finds are a rolling chance, falling per find.** The number of finds in a fight is a rolling chance. Each possible find, from the first up to the cap, rolls its own chance. The first find has the highest chance, the second less, then the third, the fourth, the fifth and the sixth, and so on. The encounter rank (D-47) is a modifier that raises the chance of each find. Most encounters are close to certain to give the first find, and have a decent but lower chance for the second. The cap is a possibility, not a guarantee. Open: the base chance per find, the modifier per rank, where the rule lives (001b or ITEM-002), and the seeded stream. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)
- **D-49 001b builds the finds chance rule (exception to D-29).** 001b builds the chance function, its config block (`data.inventory.finds_chance`), its validator and its probe, together with the cap. The debug producer and V2-ITEM-002 call the same function, so there is one reading of "how many finds". ITEM-002 may tune the numbers, but may not remove the key. Model: each find slot rolls its own chance, `p_k = clamp(base_k + rank modifier, 0, max)` in integer percent, with the stream `inventory.finds.<stage_id>.<run_count>.<situation_id>` (never `stage.settle`), one draw per slot, all `cap` draws always made. A loss or retreat has no rank and no event. The result is stored at the first draw. (Jeff, 2026-11-12: "001b builds it")
- **D-50 Chance numbers approved (PROPOSED defaults, tune after the probe).** `data.inventory.finds_chance`: `base_by_slot_pct` [85, 60, 40, 25, 15, 8]; `rank_modifier_pct` D -10, C 0, B +5, A +10, S +15; `max_pct` 98; first-find floor 60 at the lowest win rank (validator rule). The lowest win rank today is C, so D is unreachable. Slot rolls are independent (assumed from Jeff's words "each rolls"). (Jeff, 2026-11-12: "Approve as proposed")
- **D-51 Cap enforcement: refuse loudly (Option B).** One helper (for example `InventoryService.finds_cap(cfg, realm_scale)`) gives the cap. The chance function respects it. The seam `record_found_items` refuses any find beyond the cap with the reason `over_cap` and logs it. The cap counts finds offered in the event (taken and left), one seam call per event. (Jeff, 2026-11-12)

Pack size by progression stays with V2-CONTINUITY-002 and per Echo with V2-ITEM-003 (seam S5). (b) No cell limit on the cap in 001b. 001b places items in a simple row. - **D-63 Probe P2 pass line approved (closes the pass-line part of D-09).** Pooled over 18 cells (6 Callings x calm, uneasy at afraid 0.3, bold at 0.8), 2,000 seeded cases per cell, Standing 1 against Standing 9 on the same finds: M1 (first pick differs) between 15% and 60%; M3 (mean worth of the Standing 9 first pick minus the Standing 1 first pick) at least +0.5; M4 (share of differing picks where Standing 9 has the higher worth) at least 70%. If it fails, the lever is `data.inventory.pick.sense_base` (0.3 to 0.1 if M1 is low, to 0.5 if high), one tunable at a time; `worth_weight` is the fallback. If M3 or M4 fails while M1 passes, stop and report to `sr-game-designer`. Proposal by `mid-game-designer`, shown in full in the chat. (Jeff, 2026-11-13: "Approve as proposed")
