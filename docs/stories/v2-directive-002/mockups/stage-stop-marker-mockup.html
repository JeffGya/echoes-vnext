Step bar: baseline, lost steps crossed out

The bar always shows the baseline maximum. A shaken party loses steps: the diamond gets an X (a shape cue, not colour alone). Used steps are empty diamonds. Advance and the EchoBar stay clear.

Stop marker set

Same family as the stop-short tell: ground ring with a charcoal edge, a cream or accent line, a small head on a pole. Large, then at the 1280 size (top right).

Met something and spotted ahead. Met: hexagon, chevrons close in on a dot, double ring, cream. Spotted: arrow pennant, chevrons point out, dashed ray and ring on the far cell, Mist Blue. They differ in shape, direction and colour.

Portrait pulse

The ring is a shape, so it works without colour. The emotion chip (small hatched pill) is untouched. No number is shown.

On-demand why card (tap the marker; the party token does the same; no button)

Five slots at most. The cause slot can read a Directive (tint bar + glyph + name, like the badge) or a named Echo.

Marker head and ring at 1280x720

The slanting pole seen at 960x540 is gone. The ring scales with the cell and the head sits at 0.9 of the ring radius, so the pole is near vertical at 1280x720 and larger sizes. This holds only because the token and the marker head are one target (alias). If they must be two separate targets 8 units apart, the head must sit 56 units away. Then the ring needs full size, and it overhangs the cell at 1280x720.
