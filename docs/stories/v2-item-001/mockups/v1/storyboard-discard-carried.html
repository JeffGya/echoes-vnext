Before confirm. F1 and F2 use the modal clock. Modal opens at 0.00 s: backdrop 0.15 s, card fades and rises 4 units. Let it go shows at 70 percent and ignores input for 0.25 s. Esc, Back, B and an outside tap mean Keep it. No timeout, no skip.
Lost motion, end state. Body fill 0.35. Lift 8 units. Outline and shape mark stay at 1.0. QUAD ease-out. No scale, no rotation, no red, no shake, no strike. The result is true from frame 0. S1 version: 0.35 s to 0.35, then 0.15 s to 0. Selection clears at once.
Glide. Only when core moves a carried piece into the freed place. 0.4 s SINE ease-in-out, starts 0.2 s after the lost motion starts. The carried bed fades on landing. Ghost path and ghost box show the move. If core does not move it, no glide.
