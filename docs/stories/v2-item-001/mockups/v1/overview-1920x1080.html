S1 to S8 at scale 0.2. Tap marks are on the detail sheets. Existing chrome in cream or white is the game as built; the sixth rail button, count card, chip, rows and tiles are new. All new text is a mark. At 1920 the existing chrome is drawn at 1.25 (the 1536 logical canvas); item sizes follow the artist (bed 80).
