# Anti-Patterns

Use this file when a concept feels ambitious but weakly playable.

## Common Failure Modes

### Lore Standing In For Play

- Rich worldbuilding with thin decisions
- Theme carried by text only
- Emotional claims unsupported by mechanics

### Indirect Control Without Real Influence

- The player mostly watches systems resolve themselves
- Inputs are too abstract to connect to outcomes
- Autonomy is used as an excuse for weak agency

### Relationship Systems That Only Label Attachment

- Hearts, affinity, trust, or bond scores that rarely change behavior
- Characters that say they matter but remain mechanically interchangeable
- Social choices with little downstream consequence

### System Sprawl

- Too many currencies, traits, statuses, or subloops
- Several systems exist only to feed menus
- The player cannot tell what to optimize or why

### Decorative UI

- Beautiful screens that hide urgency or causality
- Mood-heavy presentation with low readability
- Excessive iconography or animation that obscures state

### Fake Replayability

- Procedural variation without meaningful strategic or emotional variation
- Large content volume with repeated decision patterns
- Multiple routes that collapse into the same dominant loop

## Rescue Prompts

When fixing a weak design, ask:

- What is the one fantasy that deserves to survive?
- What are the few decisions that must feel good repeatedly?
- What must the player notice instantly?
- What must the player remember across sessions or runs?
- Which system can be removed with little loss?
- Where should surprise come from: characters, economy, combat, story, or spatial play?
